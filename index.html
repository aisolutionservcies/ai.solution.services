Process

How the Process Works

Our process is designed to be simple, transparent and customer-friendly. Before work begins, you will know what you will receive, the expected timeframe and when payment is due.

Requirement→ Review→ Free Draft→ Confirmation→ Payment→ Final Work→ 7-Day Revision Support
  1. 01

    Tell Us Your Requirement

    Tell us your requirement or problem through WhatsApp or Gmail. The clearer the information you provide, the better we can understand your needs.

  2. 02

    Requirement Review

    We review your requirement and confirm whether it fits the selected service scope. If it differs from the listed limits, any additional cost or applicable changes are explained before work begins. No additional charge is added without your confirmation.

  3. 03

    Free Short Draft

    For each service, a short sample/draft is provided before the main work begins so you can see the proposed direction and check whether it matches your requirement.

  4. 04

    Your Confirmation

    After reviewing the draft, you can decide whether the proposed direction is satisfactory and whether you want to proceed to the final work.

  5. 05

    Payment (Paid Orders)

    For paid orders, payment is made before the final work is delivered. The applicable service, final price and agreed requirement are clearly communicated before payment.

  6. 06

    Final Work

    After payment confirmation, the final work is prepared according to your requirement and delivered through WhatsApp, Gmail or another suitable format/channel. We generally aim to deliver within 24–72 hours. Actual delivery time depends on the requirement and complexity of the work.

  7. 07

    Revision Support After Delivery

    After final delivery, you receive revision support for 7 days. Applicable revisions during this period have no additional revision charge. After 7 days, revision charges apply — see the Revision Policy.

Refund Policy: Agar delivery ke 7 dinon ke andar delivered work agreed requirement se materially match nahi karta, to refund request review ki ja sakti hai — puri details Refund Policy are available there.

Privacy & Confidentiality: Information and files you share are handled confidentially as described in the Privacy Policy and are used for providing the requested service.
