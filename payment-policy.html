Payment Policy

Last Updated: 24 September 2026

We aim to keep our payment process simple and transparent.

1. Service Price

The price displayed for a listed service is the standard final price for that service within its stated scope.

If a customer's requirement falls outside the listed scope, any additional cost will be communicated and confirmed before the additional work begins.

2. Free Short Draft

For paid services, a short draft/sample is provided before final payment so that the customer can understand the proposed direction of the work.

The free draft is a short sample, not the complete final deliverable. For a new customer, the first eligible service up to ₹500 value is free, including the draft and final delivery, subject to the offer terms shown on the website.

3. Payment Before Final Delivery

For paid orders, once the customer reviews the draft and confirms that they wish to proceed, payment is required before the final deliverable is sent.

The eligible first order for a new customer (up to ₹500 value) is free under the website offer and does not require payment.

For paid orders, the final work will be delivered after payment confirmation.

4. Payment Confirmation

The customer should retain the payment confirmation or transaction record until the service has been completed.

5. Additional Work

No additional paid work will be undertaken without informing the customer of the applicable additional charge and obtaining confirmation.

6. Failed or Unconfirmed Payments

A service will not be treated as paid until the payment has been successfully received or otherwise confirmed.

If a payment is pending, failed or reversed, final delivery may be placed on hold until the payment issue is resolved.

7. Payment and Refund Records

Relevant order, payment and communication records may be retained where reasonably necessary for service delivery, customer support, accounting, dispute handling or legal compliance, subject to our Privacy Policy.

This is website copy, not formal legal advice or a guarantee of legal protection.
